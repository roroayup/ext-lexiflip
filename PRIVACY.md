# Lexiflip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Lexiflip is a browser extension for learning vocabulary with flashcards. Cards can come from the
article you are reading, from a document you open in Lexiflip, from text you select, or from words
you type in yourself. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What stays on your device

- Your flashcard decks: words, meanings, example sentences, the title and address of the page or
  the name of the document a deck came from, and which cards you marked as learned.
- Your settings (translation language, number of cards, widget position, selection button).
- A cache of word meanings, so the same word is not looked up twice.

All of this is stored with the browser's extension storage (`storage.local`) on your device. It is
not synced to any server operated by the developer. Removing the extension deletes it.

**Documents you upload are read entirely on your device.** Their content is never sent anywhere;
only the words you then choose to turn into cards are looked up (see below).

## What is sent to third parties

To find meanings, Lexiflip sends **individual words or short phrases** that you chose (picked from a
page or document, selected on a page, or typed in), plus, when a word has no direct translation, the
short English dictionary definition of that word, one per request, to:

- **Free Dictionary API** (`api.dictionaryapi.dev`) to get English definitions and pronunciation.
- **MyMemory** (`api.mymemory.translated.net`, by Translated srl) to get translations.

Lexiflip never sends full page text, document content, page addresses, or any account, cookie or
personal identifier to these services. Requests are made without cookies. These services receive
your IP address as part of any normal web request and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Nothing is sent until you create cards or ask for a meaning (the selection popover, or
*Cari arti otomatis*).

## Permissions

- **Access to the websites you visit** is used only to show the flashcard widget on every page, to
  show the button when you select text, and to read a page's text on your device when you ask to
  make cards from it. It is also used once after an install or update to put the widget back into
  tabs that were already open.
- **Storage** keeps your decks and settings on your device.
- **Context menus** add the right-click actions.

## What Lexiflip does not do

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- No selling or sharing of data.
- No reading of pages in the background: page text is only read when you click
  *Buat dari halaman ini*, select text, or use the right-click menu.

## Ringkasan (Bahasa Indonesia)

Dek, pengaturan, dan cache arti hanya disimpan di browser kamu. Dokumen yang kamu upload dibaca di
komputer kamu sendiri dan isinya tidak dikirim ke mana pun. Untuk mencari arti, hanya kata atau
frasa yang kamu pilih atau ketik (dan, bila perlu, definisi singkatnya) yang dikirim ke Free
Dictionary API dan MyMemory. Teks lengkap halaman, isi dokumen, dan alamat halaman tidak pernah
dikirim. Tidak ada analitik, pelacakan, atau iklan.
