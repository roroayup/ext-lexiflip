# Lexiflip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Lexiflip is a browser extension that turns the article you are reading into vocabulary
flashcards. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What stays on your device

- The flashcard decks you create (words, the sentence each word came from, the page title and
  address, and which cards you marked as learned).
- Your settings (translation language, number of cards, widget position).
- A cache of word meanings, so the same word is not looked up twice.

All of this is stored with the browser's extension storage (`storage.local`) on your device. It is
not synced to any server operated by the developer. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What is sent to third parties

When you create a deck, Lexiflip reads the text of the current page **on your device** and picks
up to 50 individual words. **Only those single words** are sent, one per request, to:

- **Free Dictionary API** (`api.dictionaryapi.dev`) to get English definitions and pronunciation.
- **MyMemory** (`api.mymemory.translated.net`, by Translated srl) to get translations.

Lexiflip never sends the full page text, the page address, or any account, cookie or personal
identifier to these services. Requests are made without cookies. These services receive your IP
address as part of any normal web request and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Nothing is sent until you choose to create a deck.

## What Lexiflip does not do

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- No selling or sharing of data.
- No reading of pages in the background: page text is only read when you click
  *Buat dari halaman ini* or use the right-click menu.

## Ringkasan (Bahasa Indonesia)

Dek, pengaturan, dan cache arti hanya disimpan di browser kamu. Saat kamu membuat dek, hanya
kata-kata tunggal yang dipilih (maksimal 50) yang dikirim ke Free Dictionary API dan MyMemory
untuk mendapatkan arti. Teks lengkap halaman dan alamatnya tidak pernah dikirim. Tidak ada
analitik, pelacakan, atau iklan.
